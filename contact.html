CONTACT & VISITOR FAQ

Can parents observe daily training sessions?

expand_more
Yes. All parent guardians have access to our covered elevated observation grandstands equipped with complimentary Wi-Fi and direct line-of-sight to training pitches.

How do I arrange a private tour of the facilities?

expand_more
Simply submit the inquiry form above with the date you prefer, or call our admissions hotline directly. Campus tours are hosted Tuesday through Saturday mornings.

What safety equipment should athletes bring to evaluation?

expand_more
Athletes should arrive with molded FG/AG boots, shin guards, a personal hydration bottle, and athletic attire. GPS tracking vests and evaluation bibs are provided by the academy.
